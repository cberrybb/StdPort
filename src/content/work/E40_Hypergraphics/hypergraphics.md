Inspired by *Genshin Impact* and Studio Ghibli, this virtual production piece transitions from a medieval study into a vibrant, wind-swept field. Utilising real-time SmartStage camera tracking, the project embeds a live-action actor directly within the stylized 3D environment to blend physical performance with virtual space.

Built in Unreal Engine and Blender, the scene uses custom wind shaders and modified GLB assets for continuous camera movement. Spatial scaling, backplate synchronisation, and photogrammetry ensure the background dynamically responds to the performer's scale and positioning.
