# Motion Graphic Design

This 20-second motion graphics title sequence, inspired by Will Self’s short story *Anna* (or *iAnna*), explores themes of digital alienation and psychological isolation. It visually narrates Anna's delusion of becoming an iPad through a shaky, first-person perspective, highlighting her mounting panic with HUD overlays such as low-battery indicators.

Utilising kinetic typography and glitch effects, the design reflects Anna’s fading identity. The visual pacing is complemented by a custom soundscape featuring atmospheric synths and real-world audio, such as alarm chimes, creating an anxiety-inducing rhythm that culminates in a snap-to-black for the final title.
