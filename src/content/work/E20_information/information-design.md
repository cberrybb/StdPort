# Information Design

The multivariate poster titled *Body, Brain, Life: Mapping the Species Danger Index* investigates the quantitative and qualitative connections between biological characteristics and environmental threats across six different species: pig, fox, cow, mouse, rabbit, and cat. It visually represents biological data by aligning a central vertical comparative scale of body weight (in kg) with brain weight (in grams), complemented by segmented curved graphic symbols indicating danger indices and circular ring charts denoting maximum lifespans, calibrated to a 30-year benchmark.

Employing custom geometric line-art pictograms and colour-coded visual keys, the design achieves a balance between narrative clarity and data richness. The layout's visual flow—refined through iterative tweaks to line weights, standardisation of scales, and simplification of legends—creates a clear hierarchy that transforms abstract biological research into an engaging graphic format.
