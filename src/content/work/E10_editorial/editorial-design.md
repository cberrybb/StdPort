# Editorial Design

I created an Editorial Design project using *The Macramé of Resistance* by Lorraine Wild as the sole content, exploring her ideas around craft, knowledge, experimentation, and the role of the designer.

The project focused on using typography and composition to visually reflect the concept of craft as something built through experience and experimentation. Inspired by the idea of macramé and interconnected structures, I used repeated lines, layered typography, varied scale, and unconventional layouts to create a sense of weaving and connection throughout the publication. The limited red, black, and white colour palette creates consistency while allowing the typography and compositions to remain expressive.

Throughout the book, I experimented with hierarchy, spacing, orientation, and fragmented type to give each spread its own identity while maintaining a consistent visual system. This project strengthened my understanding of editorial design, typographic hierarchy, grid systems, and how experimental typography can visually communicate the meaning and tone of written content.
