Designed *LeafFocus*, a productivity app that helps first-year university students manage tasks, deadlines, and study routines without feeling overwhelmed. The concept was developed through user research and interviews, leading to a calming, nature-inspired interface that combines task management, calendar planning, and a visual Pomodoro timer to encourage mindful productivity.

I carried out UX research, developed wireframes, designed the user interface, created prototypes, and conducted usability testing, all with an emphasis on crafting a straightforward, intuitive experience that minimises cognitive strain and helps students establish effective study habits.
